## 👋 About me

I'm a **fullstack developer** based in **Rzeszów, Poland**, with over a year of commercial experience as a **Web Developer at Ideo**, where I build e-commerce stores – in 4 of 6 projects as the lead frontend developer, taking the whole frontend from a Figma mockup to a responsive, WCAG-friendly interface. In my own projects I work end to end: database, API, frontend, Docker setup and tests, mostly with **.NET, Laravel and Django** on the backend and **Vue, React or Angular** on the frontend.

Alongside fullstack work, I explore **machine learning in Python** – my engineering thesis was a recommendation system with six methods implemented from scratch.

- 🎓 **Master's degree in Computer Science** – University of Rzeszów (in progress, semester 2 of 3)
- 🎓 **Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science** – University of Rzeszów (completed)
- 🏆 Rector's scholarship for academic results in three consecutive years of the engineering programme
- 💼 **Open to new opportunities**
- 🧰 Main stacks: **ASP.NET Core, Laravel, Django** · **Vue, React, Angular**
- 🤖 Interested in practical **ML**: recommendations, classification, data analysis
- 📫 Best way to reach me: **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/piotr-smo%C5%82a-181aab344/)**

## 💼 Experience

- **Web Developer – Ideo Sp. z o.o., Rzeszów** (Sep 2025 – present) – e-commerce stores on the Edito 4 CMS: Blade and SCSS views, Vue.js components in TypeScript, simpler backend tasks in PHP and MySQL, teamwork in Redmine and GitLab, from estimation to handover to QA and the client
- **PHP Developer Intern – Ideo Sp. z o.o., Rzeszów** (May – Jun 2025) – Edito 4 CMS modules and Laravel applications with React and Vue frontends consuming REST APIs, integrations with external APIs
